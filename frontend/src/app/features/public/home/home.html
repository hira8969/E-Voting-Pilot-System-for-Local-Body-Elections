Frequently Asked Questions

Citizen Assistance & Queries

Who is eligible to vote in this local election?

Any Indian citizen aged 18 or above whose name is enrolled in the local ward voter register with an active Voter ID.

Can I change my vote once cast on the EVM?

No. As per Election Commission regulations, once a vote is confirmed and recorded into the secure digital ledger, it cannot be altered.

Will my vote selection appear on the receipt?

No. To preserve absolute ballot secrecy and prevent voter coercion, receipts contain only your cryptographic transaction stamp and booth reference.
