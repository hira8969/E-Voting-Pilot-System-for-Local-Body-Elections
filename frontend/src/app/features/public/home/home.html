Simple 6-Step Workflow

How Online Voting Works

A seamless and verifiable democratic process designed for ease of use and strict electoral compliance.

01

Register

Create your account with your Full Name, Email, and registered Voter ID (EPIC) or Student ID.

02

Verify Account

Authenticate securely through multi-factor validation or verified credentials before accessing ballots.

03

View Eligible Election

Access your assigned elections based on your verified constituency, ward, or institution.

04

Select Candidate

Review candidates, their affiliated party, symbol, and manifesto, then select your chosen candidate.

05

Review Vote

Carefully verify your candidate selection on the explicit review screen before final submission.

06

Confirm Vote

Submit your confirmed vote. Your digital ballot is cryptographically sealed and recorded in the audit trail.
