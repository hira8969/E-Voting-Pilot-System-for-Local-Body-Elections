Frequently Asked Questions

Is my vote choice visible to system administrators or returning officers?

No. The election architecture cryptographically separates voter identity from the cast ballot. While the system marks your record as "Voted" to prevent duplicate voting, your individual candidate choice is never linked to your identity in the database or server logs.

What happens if my network connection drops during voting?

Your vote is only committed when you tap "Confirm Vote" in the final review modal. If disconnected before confirmation, your voter record remains open. If disconnected after confirmation, the backend idempotency key ensures your vote is safely registered once without duplicates.

How can I check the results after polling closes?

Official aggregated counts and ward-wise turnout become publicly accessible on the Public Results Dashboard once counting is certified by the Returning Officer.
