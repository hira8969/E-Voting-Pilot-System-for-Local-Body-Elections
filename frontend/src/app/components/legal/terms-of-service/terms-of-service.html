DRAFT TERMS OF SERVICE — TECHNICAL PILOT SPECIFICATION These terms govern use of the digital voting pilot platform. Any deployment in legal local body elections must be promulgated under statutory rules framed by the State Legislature and Election Commission.

Electoral Platform Terms of Service

Version 1.0 (Pilot System Draft) • Effective: October 2026

1. Scope of the Demonstration System

This platform represents a prototype electronic voting and election administration pilot. It enables authorized voters, candidates, party representatives, and electoral officers to evaluate digital balloting, nomination scrutiny, audit trail generation, and certified tally computation.

2. Voter Eligibility and One-Vote Rule

Participation in an election is restricted to electors who meet the verified electoral roll criteria for the corresponding ward or constituency:

  • Each registered voter is entitled to cast exactly one ballot per election event.
  • The backend service enforces duplicate voting prevention. Any secondary submission attempt is rejected automatically with a 409 Conflict status.
  • Attempting to cast votes on behalf of another citizen or submitting fraudulent identity tokens is strictly prohibited.

3. Irrevocability of Confirmed Ballots

Prior to final submission, voters are provided an explicit ballot review dialogue to inspect their candidate selection. Once confirmed and sealed into the cryptographic tally, the ballot is final and cannot be cancelled, amended, or retrieved by any person, including the voter or system administrators.

4. Acceptable System Use and Security Obligations

Users must not probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the system without authorization, attempt denial-of-service attacks, or reverse-engineer the cryptographic tally components. Vulnerabilities discovered during security reviews should be submitted in compliance with our Responsible Disclosure Policy.

5. Disclaimer and Statutory Certifications

UI and code enhancements do not independently constitute statutory certification. Full deployment for official binding elections requires comprehensive independent third-party penetration testing, algorithmic tally verification, and legal gazette notification.
