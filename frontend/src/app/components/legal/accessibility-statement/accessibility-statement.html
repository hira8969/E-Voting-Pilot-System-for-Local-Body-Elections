Accessibility & Universal Inclusivity Statement

Compliance Target: WCAG 2.2 Level AA • Last Updated: October 2026

1. Commitment to Accessible Civic Participation

Every eligible citizen has a democratic right to vote privately and independently. This digital election platform is engineered according to the principles of Perceivability, Operability, Understandability, and Robustness (POUR).

2. Implemented Accessibility Features

High Contrast & Readable Typography

Tested against 4.5:1 minimum contrast ratios for body copy (#253247 on #FCFCFA) and 3:1 for graphical UI elements and form borders (#DCE2E8). Clean Source Sans 3 typography with comfortable proportional leading.

Full Keyboard Navigation

Every interactive control, ballot radio choice, modal action, and table navigation element is reachable and operable using Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, and Spacebar, accompanied by distinct 2px focus indicators.

Screen Reader Semantic Hierarchy

Proper HTML5 landmarks (<main>, <nav>, <header>, <footer>, <article>), single <h1> hierarchy, descriptive alt tags, and ARIA labels on modal overlays and live regions.

Respect for Reduced Motion

All CSS transitions and animations are suppressed automatically when users have set prefers-reduced-motion: reduce in their operating system preferences.

3. Neutral and Non-Biased Candidate Presentation

To avoid perceptual priming or accidental ballot bias:

  • All candidates on the EVM ballot are rendered with uniform rectangular geometry, identical padding, and equal typography sizing.
  • No candidate is highlighted with primary action colors, glowing borders, or preferential visual weight before voter selection.
  • Selected choices are communicated through dual indicators (checkbox marker + text label "Selected") rather than color alone.

4. Accessibility Feedback and Remediation

If you encounter any barrier while accessing elections or ballots on assistive technology, please notify our accessibility coordinator through the Support Desk. We prioritize accessibility bug reports for immediate technical remediation.
