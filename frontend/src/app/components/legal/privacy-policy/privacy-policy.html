DRAFT POLICY DOCUMENT — SUBJECT TO STATUTORY REVIEW This privacy policy is a technical pilot draft prepared for this demonstration election system. Before deployment in any statutory or official public election, this policy must be audited, amended, and approved by qualified legal counsel, government data protection authorities, and the State Election Commission.

Electoral Data Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 (Pilot System Specification) • Effective Date: October 2026

1. Principle of Ballot Secrecy and Voter Anonymity

The core constitutional mandate of this election system is the absolute decoupling of voter identity from cast ballots. When a voter submits an electronic ballot:

  • The ballot choice is cryptographically encrypted and cast directly into the aggregate tally ledger.
  • The voter's registration record is marked solely with a status flag indicating participation to prevent duplicate voting.
  • No system database table, application memory cache, server log, URL parameter, or network telemetry packet links the citizen's identity (such as Voter ID or National ID) with their candidate selection.

2. Information Collected for Voter Roll Scrutiny

To establish voter eligibility in municipal wards and local bodies, the system processes:

  • Electoral Roll Identifiers: Official Voter ID (EPIC) number, full registered legal name, and assigned constituency/ward.
  • Authentication Credentials: Secure hashed passwords (using salted bcrypt) and temporary one-time passcodes (OTP) for session verification.
  • Audit Trail Data: Timestamp of system login, timestamp of ballot submission (recorded without ballot payload), and terminal session status.

3. Role-Based Access Control and Data Retention

Administrative personnel, returning officers, and observers possess strictly partitioned privileges:

  • Election Officers can verify citizen voter roll entries but cannot view ballots or alter tally tallies.
  • Independent Auditors access immutable audit events and system health logs in read-only mode.
  • Session tokens expire automatically after periods of inactivity to protect shared polling terminals.

4. Third-Party Sharing and Analytics

This election platform does not sell, license, or transmit voter information to commercial marketing entities, advertising networks, or third-party tracking providers. No external tracking pixels or invasive client analytics scripts are loaded in this application.

5. Grievances and Inquiries

Citizens with queries concerning voter registration records or privacy rights may contact the pilot grievance desk via the Contact and Support Portal.
