Electoral System Service Topology

Each microservice encapsulates a dedicated electoral bounded context, backed by an isolated database schema to enforce domain separation and high data integrity.

Identity & Electoral Roll

Auth Service manages JWT verification and RBAC roles; Voter Service manages citizen EPIC validation and eligibility status.

Election Operations

Election Service orchestrates schedules and constituencies; Candidate Service handles nominations, party symbols, and approval scrutiny.

Balloting & Auditing

Voting Service enforces one-voter-one-vote and produces digital receipts; Result Service tallies outcomes; Audit Service records immutable logs.
